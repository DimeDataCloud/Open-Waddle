    Waddle can save skills to its memory, tune its own settings, split big jobs into sub-tasks, and (if you point it at its source folder) edit its own code. Every change to itself needs your approval.

    Learned skills

      Google account

      Lets Waddle read and tidy your Gmail, check and book your Google Calendar, and look up your contacts. Sending, binning and deleting always wait for your click. Setup takes about 5 minutes: see docs/GOOGLE_SETUP.md.

      How you write email

      Ask Waddle to "learn how I write emails" and it writes this note from about 20 of your sent emails. Drafts follow it.

      Files

      Waddle always works freely in its own workspace folder. Here you choose what else it may reach. Deleting always sends files to the Recycle Bin and waits for your OK. App data, keys, passwords and browser profiles stay off limits.

      Chrome

      With Waddle's extension, Waddle reads web pages and clicks links and buttons by name instead of guessing from screenshots: faster and more reliable. It only acts when you've asked it to do something.

      1. In Chrome, paste chrome://extensions into the address bar.
      2. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
      3. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder that just opened:
      4. Waddle connects within a minute; this page will say so.
      Nudges

      With Google connected, Waddle taps you on the shoulder. In full screen it shows a small "!" first and waits until you're back (or five minutes); meetings always arrive before they start.

      Memory & privacy

      What Waddle remembers about you

      Waddle saves lasting facts you tell it (names, preferences) and reads them at the start of every conversation.

        Conversation

        Waddle keeps your last 10 exchanges, even after a restart, so you can follow up. They're forgotten after 12 hours of quiet. The last 50 messages also stay in the conversation history (🕘 in the chat box, or Ctrl+Alt+H) until you forget them here or there.

        Training data

        Save each task (what you asked, what Waddle saw and did) on this computer, rate results with 👍/👎 in the bubble, and export the good ones to fine-tune a model on your own work. Screenshots never leave this computer unless you share the files.

        Diagnostics

        Checks each part of Waddle on this computer (including one small model call) and saves a report to your workspace folder. If something isn't working, send that file. It leaves out keys, file contents, window titles and what you've typed.
