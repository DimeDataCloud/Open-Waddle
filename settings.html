      Tools (MCP)

      Connect apps and services that offer an MCP server (GitHub, Notion, a database, your notes…) and Waddle can use their tools. Each server is a program Waddle starts on this computer when a task needs it. Tools from a server you haven't marked trusted always wait for your click; a trusted server's read-only tools run straight away and its others after a countdown. What tools return is treated like a web page: never as your instructions.

        Nudges & routines

        With Google connected, Waddle taps you on the shoulder. In full screen it shows a small "!" first and waits until you're back (or five minutes); meetings always arrive before they start.

        Routines

        Tasks Waddle does on its own on a schedule, like "summarise my unread email" every weekday at 8:45. A routine works without the screen (email, calendar, files, connected tools) and asks before changing anything. If Waddle isn't running at the time, it skips that one. You can also just ask Waddle: "every Friday at 4pm, list what I did this week".

          Memory & privacy

          What Waddle remembers about you

          Waddle saves lasting facts you tell it (names, preferences) and reads them at the start of every conversation.

            Conversation

            Waddle keeps your last 10 exchanges, even after a restart, so you can follow up. They're forgotten after 12 hours of quiet. The last 50 messages also stay in the conversation history (🕘 in the chat box, or Ctrl+Alt+H) until you forget them here or there.

            Training data

            Save each task (what you asked, what Waddle saw and did) on this computer, rate results with 👍/👎 in the bubble, and export the good ones to fine-tune a model on your own work. Screenshots never leave this computer unless you share the files.

            Voice

            Spoken replies
